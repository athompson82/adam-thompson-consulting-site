✦ Before you start

Working with an AI assistant

AI can turn a messy export into a finished report in minutes. Whether that report is right depends on you. Three habits make the difference:

1Be specificSay exactly what you want: the format, the columns, the categories. It can’t read your mind, or your manager’s.
2One job at a timeSmaller requests give you changes you can actually see and check.
3Check before you trustAI is fast and confident, but not always right. Verify against the data before anything goes out.
You’ll be scored on choosing strong prompts and catching the AI’s mistakes.
Message from Dana, your managerCan you send me October sales by category before Friday? Just use the store export. Thanks!
