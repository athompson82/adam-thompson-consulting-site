Program redesignCommercial banking

Turning a 4.5-day workshop into a one-hour course

The needAdvisors needed the judgement from a multi-day case without a week away from clients.

  • StrategyIdentified the decisions that mattered most and cut the rest.
  • DesignBuilt a three-step decision framework into four units that follow the real client workflow.
  • BuildRise course with scenario checks and a Storyline assessment.

OutcomeThe core of a 4.5-day program in about an hour of learner time.

Case seriesHealthcare banking

Practice cases for advising healthcare practices

The needAdvisors needed safe practice with a specialised client group before meeting them for real.

  • StrategyPaired self-paced cases with a live workshop for the most complex clients.
  • DesignThree fictional practices, each with 5–8 key decisions and a just-in-time coach.
  • BuildOne reusable Storyline framework across every case, with French to follow.

OutcomeAdvisors rehearse real conversations, and the framework scales to new cases.

Course seriesCommercial banking

Financial analysis for relationship managers

The needNew relationship managers and interns needed to read financials like experienced lenders.

  • DesignFour scenarios built around fictional companies with realistic statements.
  • BuildInteractive modules on cash flow, ratios, EBITDA and lending risk signals.

OutcomeAnalysis practised on realistic companies, not abstract formulas.

Course + videoFinancial services

A negotiation program that works and sticks

The needA large course had to run reliably in the LMS and stay useful after launch.

  • BuildFixed tracking and resume issues and ran a full QA pass.
  • DesignA cinematic video that brings the negotiation framework to life.
  • SupportBilingual, accessible guides and one-page job aids.

OutcomeA dependable course plus tools people keep using after it ends.
